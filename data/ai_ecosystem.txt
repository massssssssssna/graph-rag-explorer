A Structured Reference Guide for Knowledge Graph Construction & Triple Extraction

1. Introduction to the AI Knowledge Graph
This document provides an educational analysis of the modern Artificial Intelligence (AI) ecosystem. To facilitate automated Information Extraction (IE), Named Entity Recognition (NER), and Relation Extraction (RE), the text is structured using clear, declarative, and factual sentences. Every major assertion corresponds directly to a canonical subject-predicate-object triple suitable for graph database ingestion. 

Knowledge graphs represent factual data as nodes (entities) and directed edges (relations). In the domain of Artificial Intelligence, tracking how companies, models, hardware providers, and founders interact is crucial for competitive intelligence and multi-hop reasoning engines. 

Core Schema Definition
Primary Entity Classes: Company, Person, AI_Model, Hardware_Product, Software_Product.
Canonical Relations: develops, invests_in, partners_with, founded_by, powers, competes_with, releases.

7. Multi-Hop Reasoning & Query Analysis
Knowledge graphs excel at answering complex, multi-hop queries that require traversing multiple directed relationships. Below are structured breakdowns of multi-hop reasoning pathways derived from our extracted ecosystem triples. 

Multi-Hop Query Case 1: Financial & Development Lineage
Question: Which company invested in the company that developed ChatGPT?
Graph Traversal Steps:
Step 1: (?Company_X, develops, ChatGPT) -> Evaluates to: [OpenAI]
Step 2: (?Investor, invests_in, OpenAI) -> Evaluates to: [Microsoft] 
Final Answer: Microsoft (via its multi-billion dollar capital and infrastructure investment in OpenAI).
